# Home page PageSpeed Insights, 2026-11-17

What was measured, why the home page scored what it did, what the theme now does about it, and what is left that the
theme cannot change. Every cause below was measured on the live page (Playwright on Chromium, local Lighthouse 13.5,
the live HTML and CDN responses), not read off the report alone.

## Causes

1. **Layout shift (CLS 0.524 desktop, 0.24 mobile; also the only Agentic Browsing failure).** On 2026-11-16 the
   header was changed to one row with a search icon through the theme editor's Custom CSS
   (`platform_customizations.custom_css` in `config/settings_data.json`). Shopify prints that CSS as a `<style>` at the
   very end of `<body>`. The browser paints the page before the parser gets there, so the first frame showed the old
   two-row header (145 px desktop, 124 px mobile); when the style arrived the header dropped to 92 / 68 px and the
   whole page moved up. It is a race, so the size varies by run. With the same CSS moved into `<head>` the shift went
   from 0.551 to 0.0004 on desktop and from 0.243 to 0.0010 on mobile.
2. **Smaller shifts.** The slider's control bar was `display: none` until its script ran, and slides 2-3 joined the
   shared grid cell then (+6 px). With the Pegasus fonts delayed, the hero headline re-wrapped on arrival (its width was
   `14ch`, which depends on the font), up to 0.11 on mobile.
3. **Speed Index 7.3 s on mobile.** The slider changed slides every 7 s from the page load. Lighthouse's mobile trace
   runs past 7 s, so the last frame showed slide 2 and every earlier frame counted as unfinished (the simulated Speed
   Index weights the observed one 1.4 times). Real visitors who do not touch the page also got slide 2's photo as their
   largest paint.
4. **LCP 5.3 s, FCP 2.0 s on mobile.** Lighthouse simulates a slow 4G phone from a fast load: every request that
   finished before the largest paint is charged on the slow network. Far too much finished early:
   - fonts: 232 KB, mostly the display font's optical-size axis (132 KB alone);
   - 39 stylesheets (~55 KB, plus about 1.1 KB of headers each), five of them printed once per product card (24 times
     each, 120 extra `<link>` tags), four of those unused by the cards;
   - the closed cart drawer's five stylesheets, render-blocking in `<head>`;
   - the hero's own CSS sitting in the body and holding up the parser;
   - the hero photo at 88 KB, the 200 px header emblem for a 52 px spot;
   - Shopify's sign-in pop-up (`<shopify-account>`): 67 requests and about 256 KiB (Shop login, a Shop Pay iframe and
     bundle, a 64 KB font);
   - slides 2-3 photos (222 KB) and every product card's second and third photo (about 32 photos), all hidden.
5. **Accessibility.** The statement's dim words were at 0.14 opacity (1.33:1, large text needs 3:1); the constellation's
   inactive star names at 0.5 (4.34:1, needs 4.5:1); the footer's giant wordmark at 0.15 (1.46:1; `aria-hidden` does
   not exempt it from the check); the footer policy links were 18.7 px tall on desktop (24 px needed). Not flagged yet
   but wrong: 30 duplicate ids in the two product shelves, which also made the second shelf's tabs switch the first
   shelf's panels.
6. **Best Practices.** Console errors: Shopify's analytics endpoint rejects Lighthouse's user agent ("Invalid agent")
   from the Shop Pay bundle that the sign-in pop-up loads.

2026-11-17, from this office's network (its route to Shopify is slow: the same page's mobile FCP is 3.5 s here and 2.0 s on
PSI, so mobile numbers here are pessimistic). The preview also loads Shopify's preview bar frame and hot-reload script,
which the live site does not.

| Desktop before | 70 | 97 | 77* | 100 | 3/4 | 1.02 s | 1.11 s | 1.80 s | 0.55 |
| Desktop after | **97** | **100** | 77* | 100 | **4/4** | 0.96 s | 0.96 s | 1.11 s | 0 |

\* Best Practices here fails on `shop.app` third-party cookies and issues that only appear from this network; PSI's

Checks on the dev theme (Playwright, Chromium and WebKit, 1350 x 940 and 412 x 823): load CLS 0.0015 / 0; the largest paint
is the hero photo; no slide 2-3 photos and no card photos 2-3 before interaction; the first scroll arms the slider and it
moves on after 7 s; the second shelf's tabs switch their own panels; no duplicate ids; the footer wordmark is drawn by CSS;
the slider keeps the same height whichever font is showing (1440 to 390 px). Each Pegasus font downloads once (66, 41 and
23 KB). PSI on the live site after the push is the measurement that counts for mobile.

## If mobile stays under 95

In order of expected gain:
1. Load the CSS of sections further down the page without blocking (`media="print"` swap, chosen by `section.index`),
   keeping the first two sections blocking.
2. Render the inactive tabs of the two shelves on demand (Section Rendering API) instead of in the HTML.
3. Inline the first screen's CSS and load the rest after the first paint.
4. Take the fonts' preloads out (they then arrive after the first paint, at the cost of a font swap).

## Re-measuring

- Local Lighthouse (same engine as PSI): `node <lighthouse>/cli/index.js <url> --output=json --output-path=stdout`
  (mobile is the default; `--preset=desktop` for desktop), three runs, median.
- Dev theme preview without the preview bar: open
  `https://pegasuspen.com/?preview_theme_id=<theme id>&pb=0` once to get the preview cookie, then measure `/` with it.
- Layout shifts with their sources: a `PerformanceObserver` for `layout-shift` (see the Playwright checks in this
  round's notes), with the fonts delayed by 2.5 s as a second pass.
